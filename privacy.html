Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-03

FitBite (“we”) respects your privacy. This policy explains how the app / site handles information when recommending nearby meals.

What we collect

  • Location: only with your permission, to search nearby restaurants. We do not track you in the background.
  • Goals you enter: weight, calories, protein, budget, diet — stored on device by default.
  • Meal log: today’s eaten items stay on device to compute remaining budget.
  • Purchases & usage: anonymous device ID, daily location lookups, and Pro status are stored on our server to enforce free limits and subscription state.
  • Dish feedback (optional): “on menu / not on menu” and taste marks stay on device and may sync to the server to improve ranking.
  • Menu photos: preview stays on device; metadata (name/size) may stay on device when you add real dishes.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t sell your personal data
  • Free core features don’t require an account
  • Nearby search mainly calls public map APIs on your device; precise coordinates are not written to our business database by default

Third parties

Nearby places may come from OpenStreetMap / Photon and similar public map services. Payments are handled by the Apple App Store. See those services’ privacy policies too.
